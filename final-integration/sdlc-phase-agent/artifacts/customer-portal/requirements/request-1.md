Client: Acme Corp | Project: Customer Portal

## Description:

Create a customer feedback system that allows users to submit ratings and comments about our products

## Basic Requirements:

- Users can submit a rating between 1 and 5 stars for any product listed in the portal.  
- Users can add an optional comment of up to 500 characters when submitting a rating.  
- The system must validate that a rating is selected before allowing submission; if not, an error message is displayed.  
- Submitted ratings and comments are stored in the database and associated with the user’s account and the specific product.  
- The portal displays the average rating and the most recent five comments for each product on its detail page.
